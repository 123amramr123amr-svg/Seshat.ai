# ---------- Egypt & history ----------
Q: what is the longest river in the world | longest river | the nile | how long is the nile
A: The Nile is one of the longest rivers in the world. It is about 6,650 kilometers long and flows through Egypt, Sudan, and other African countries.

Q: who built the great pyramid | who built the pyramids | the great pyramid | tell me about the pyramids
A: The Great Pyramid of Giza was built for King Khufu about 4,500 years ago. It is the only wonder of the ancient world that still stands.

Q: what is the sphinx | the sphinx | tell me about the sphinx
A: The Great Sphinx is a huge limestone statue in Giza with the body of a lion and the head of a human.

Q: who was tutankhamun | tutankhamun | king tut
A: Tutankhamun was a young Egyptian king. He became famous when Howard Carter discovered his nearly intact tomb in 1922.

Q: what is the rosetta stone | rosetta stone | who decoded hieroglyphs
A: The Rosetta Stone was found in 1799. It helped Champollion decode the ancient Egyptian hieroglyphs.

Q: what are hieroglyphs | hieroglyphs | hieroglyphics | ancient egyptian writing
A: Hieroglyphs are the writing system of ancient Egypt. They use pictures and symbols instead of letters.

Q: who was cleopatra | cleopatra
A: Cleopatra VII was the last queen of Ptolemaic Egypt, known for her intelligence and strong character.

Q: who was ramses the second | ramses | ramesses ii
A: Ramses II was one of the greatest kings of ancient Egypt. He ruled for about 66 years and built the Abu Simbel temples.

Q: what is the suez canal | suez canal | what does the suez canal connect
A: The Suez Canal is a waterway in Egypt that connects the Red Sea with the Mediterranean Sea. It opened in 1869.

Q: what is the population of egypt | population of egypt | how many people live in egypt
A: Egypt has more than 100 million people, the largest population in the Arab world.

Q: what is the currency of egypt | currency of egypt | egyptian currency
A: The currency of Egypt is the Egyptian pound.

Q: where is egypt | which continent is egypt in | egypt location
A: Egypt is in the northeast of Africa, and the Sinai Peninsula is in Asia.

Q: who were the pharaohs | pharaohs | ancient egyptians
A: The pharaohs were the kings of ancient Egypt, one of the oldest civilizations in the world, more than five thousand years old.

Q: what is a mummy | mummy | mummification
A: A mummy is a body preserved after death. Ancient Egyptians used mummification to protect the body for the afterlife.
